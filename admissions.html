Admission information such as availability, placement requirements, fees and required documents will be confirmed by the school and updated here as official information becomes available.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear path to admission.

The admission journey will be kept simple, transparent and easy for parents and guardians to understand.

01

Explore

Review the available educational pathways and identify the section that best matches the learner.

03

Confirm

Confirm programme availability, placement, requirements, fees and other official admission information with the school.

Begin
